About

Kim Sohee currently teaches in the Department of Fashion Styling at Dong-Ah Institute of Media and Arts.
She previously taught at Sookmyung Women’s University and several other institutions.
Prior to her academic career, she worked as a fashion designer and operated her own fashion retail business.
